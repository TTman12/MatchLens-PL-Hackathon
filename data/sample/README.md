# Sample match: Ashmere Athletic v Brackenford

Three hand-scripted minutes of a fictional derby (match `m017`, matchday 9), for building
the stats engine, agents and player before the simulator is ready. Generated by
[`scripts/make_sample_match.py`](../../scripts/make_sample_match.py); don't edit these
files by hand, change the script and rerun it. A test fails if they drift apart.

Brackenford (`brk`) start the day top on 15 points, Ashmere (`ash`) second on 13. Ashmere
attack toward pitch `x = 105`.

## Files

| File | Contract | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `league.json` | `League` | Four fictional clubs with full squads |
| `season.json` | `Season` | Matchdays 1 to 8 played (seeded results model), table, fixtures, player season stats |
| `match.json` | `MatchMeta` | Lineups, stakes, pre-match win probability |
| `events.json` | `MatchEvent` | 51 events, attacking frame |
| `frames.jsonl` | `Frame` | 1,801 frames at 10 Hz, pitch frame, 0:00 to 3:00 |
| `moments.json` | `Moment` | Three moments at different stages: detected, and two scheduled with drafts, verdicts (one repaired, one template fallback) and director decisions |
| `cues.json` | `CueTrack` | Speed badge, replays, Player boxes for three contributors, Club and Pundit boxes, a Spanish box, the card's Club box and a timeline item |

The moments and cues are written by hand to show what the agents should produce. Their
numbers are computed from the events, frames and season, so the evidence is true.
