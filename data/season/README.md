# Synthetic season

The fictional league and its season up to the featured matchday, generated by the
simulator (`matchlens-sim season --out data/season --matchday 30`, season seed 2026).

- `league.json`: the 20 clubs, squads and player ratings (`League` contract).
- `season.json`: table, form, fixtures with scores, and player season stats (`Season`).
- `results.csv`: one row per played match.
- `featured.json`: the featured fixture (Ashmere v Brackenford, matchday 30), its seed
  and the players suspended for it.

Everything here is invented. See [docs/simulator.md](../../docs/simulator.md).
